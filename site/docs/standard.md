The normative text is [`spec/`](https://github.com/Catalyst-Forge-LLC/appledger/tree/main/spec) in the repository, format 0.1.0. Schemas are beside it in [`schemas/`](https://github.com/Catalyst-Forge-LLC/appledger/tree/main/schemas).

The specification and those schemas are CC0. The checker in this repository is MIT. Pinned copies of child xFacts schemas keep their own MIT license.

This website is not the specification. It is not deployed. [`llms.txt`](https://github.com/Catalyst-Forge-LLC/appledger/blob/main/site/static/llms.txt) in the repository points at the specification. It does not embed the specification.

An openly published specification is not certification by a standards body.
